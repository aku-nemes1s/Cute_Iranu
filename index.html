About

◈

This is an archive. A place where photographs live alongside the words and feelings that surrounded the moment of taking them.

Not a portfolio. Not a social feed. Something quieter — a record for the self, shared with whoever finds it.

The images here were made with a camera, sometimes a phone, sometimes film. They were not optimized. They were kept because something about them stayed.

Based in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia
Shooting since 2018
Preferred light Golden hour, overcast
